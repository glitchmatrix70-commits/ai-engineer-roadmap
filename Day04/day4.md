# Interview Questions & Answers

## Question 1

### What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

**Interview Answer (1–2 lines):**  
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making.

**Real-life Example:**  
Like Google Maps suggesting the fastest route or Netflix recommending movies based on your preferences.

---

## Question 2

### Difference between AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI

| Technology | Description | Example |
|------------|-------------|---------|
| **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** | A broad field focused on building machines that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence. | Self-driving cars, virtual assistants |
| **Machine Learning (ML)** | A subset of AI where computers learn patterns from data instead of being explicitly programmed. | Spam email detection |
| **Deep Learning (DL)** | A subset of ML that uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns. | Face recognition, speech recognition |
| **Generative AI (GenAI)** | AI that creates new content such as text, images, code, audio, or videos. | ChatGPT, Midjourney, DALL·E |

**Real-life Example:**  
AI is the entire hospital.  
Machine Learning is one department.  
Deep Learning is a specialized ICU.  
Generative AI is a doctor who can write reports, create images, or generate treatment suggestions.

## Question 3

### What is an LLM?

**Interview Answer (1–2 lines):**  
A Large Language Model (LLM) is an AI model trained on massive amounts of text data to understand and generate human-like language.

**Real-life Example:**  
Like a librarian who has read millions of books and can answer questions or write content based on that knowledge.

## Question 5

### What is the difference between Google Search and ChatGPT?

| Google Search | ChatGPT |
|---------------|----------|
| Searches the internet for existing web pages. | Generates answers using a trained language model. |
| Provides links to websites. | Provides direct conversational responses. |
| Best for finding current information. | Best for explanations, coding, writing, brainstorming, and learning. |

**Real-life Example:**  
Google is like a librarian who tells you which book to read.  
ChatGPT is like a teacher who explains the answer in simple words.

---

## Question 6

### Why does ChatGPT sometimes make mistakes (hallucinate)?

**Interview Answer (1–2 lines):**  
ChatGPT predicts the most likely next words based on patterns in its training data. Since it doesn't truly understand facts or verify every response, it can sometimes generate incorrect or fabricated information, known as hallucinations.

**Real-life Example:**  
Like a student confidently answering a question by guessing instead of checking the textbook.

---

## Question 7

### What does an AI Engineer do?

**Interview Answer:**

- Builds AI-powered applications and products.
- Integrates AI models using APIs.
- Designs prompts and AI workflows.
- Fine-tunes, evaluates, and deploys AI models.
- Connects AI with databases, APIs, and other software.
- Monitors, improves, and maintains AI systems in production.

# 📝 Notes (Day04/notes.md)

## Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence is the field of creating machines that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, and decision-making.

---

## Machine Learning (ML)

Machine Learning is a branch of AI where computers learn patterns from data to make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed.

---

## Deep Learning (DL)

Deep Learning is a type of Machine Learning that uses multi-layered neural networks to solve complex problems like image recognition and language understanding.

---

## Generative AI (GenAI)

Generative AI is a type of AI that creates new content such as text, images, videos, music, or code based on user prompts.

---

## LLM (Large Language Model)

A Large Language Model is an AI model trained on vast amounts of text data to understand and generate human-like language.

---

## Token

A token is the smallest unit of text processed by an LLM. It can be a word, part of a word, punctuation mark, or symbol.

**Example:**  
`"ChatGPT is awesome!"` may be broken into tokens like:

- Chat
- GPT
- is
- awesome
- !

---

## Prompt

A prompt is the instruction or question given to an AI model to generate a response.

**Example:**

> "Write a Python function to calculate factorial."

---

## Hallucination

Hallucination occurs when an AI generates information that sounds correct but is actually false, misleading, or made up.

**Example:**  
ChatGPT inventing a fake research paper or citing a source that doesn't exist.

---

# 🚀 Quick Revision

- **AI** → Machines performing human-like intelligent tasks.
- **ML** → AI that learns from data.
- **DL** → ML using deep neural networks.
- **GenAI** → AI that creates new content.
- **LLM** → Large Language Model trained on massive text datasets.
- **Token** → Smallest unit of text processed by an LLM.
- **Prompt** → Input given to an AI model.
- **Hallucination** → AI generating confident but incorrect information.
